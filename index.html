The Last Violin

You carry a sword and the last violin in the land. The Hush follows you, reaching with eyes in its palms. Gather the scattered notes, defeat each tower’s guardian, and play. Every melody costs The Hush a finger.

Tip: tap Play full screen to open the game in its own tab, then turn your phone sideways. Or tap the ⟳ button at the top to turn the game sideways right here.

Your progress and sound setting are saved in this browser.

  • Keep moving right. Standing still, backing up, or getting stuck lets the beast close in. Watch the distance bar.
  • Every level holds 88 notes ♪. The tower door opens only when you have gathered the number shown at the top left.
  • Hearts: lava, pits, enemies, falling spikes and guardian attacks cost one heart. Swinging iron balls cost half. Glowing heart lanterns restore one. Many guardians drop a Potion of Life that adds a heart.
  • Never let The Hush catch you. If it reaches you, the game is over.
  • Powers: the Scroll of Consonance (level 5) gives a 15-second golden shield once per level, but not in guardian fights. The Harmonic Boots (level 7) let you jump again in mid-air.
  • Hydra and the Kraken shrug off your staff’s golden bolts and sometimes raise a shield. Strike them up close with the staff itself, and wait out the shield.
  • Falling to a guardian? You can retry the fight, but you come back with half your hearts.
  • New Game X unlocks when you finish the game: monsters have double health, every note is worth double, and you start with the golden violin, Consonance and the Harmonic Boots.
  • Score: every note adds 10 points. Every half heart you lose takes 5 away.
  • Falling spikes shake before they drop. Webs hold you for two seconds. Iron balls can be passed by sliding.
  • A guardian blocks the way before every tower. Watch for its warning glow, then strike. Some can only be hurt at certain moments.
  • On a phone or tablet, hold the arrow and jump buttons at the same time to run and jump. Turning the device sideways gives the biggest view.
  • Controllers (Xbox, PlayStation, or any controller made for iPhone) work too. The on-screen buttons hide while you use one; touch the screen to bring them back. In menus, move with the D-pad or stick, press A to choose and B to go back.
  • Hold → for 3 seconds to run automatically. Press ← to stop.
